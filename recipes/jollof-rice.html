Description

Jollof rice is a Popular West African rice dish cooked with tomatoes,peppers,onions and spices.

Ingredients
  • Rice
  • Tomatoes
  • Onions
  • peppers
  • cooking oil
  • Salt
  • Spices

Steps How it Prepare.
  1. Wash the rice.
  2. prepare the tomato and pepper mixture.
  3. Cook the mixture with the spices.
  4. Add the rice and water.
  5. Cook untill the rice is tender.
  6. Serve the jollof rice.
