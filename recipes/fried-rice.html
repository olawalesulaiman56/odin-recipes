Fried Rice

Home

Description

African fried rice is a colorful and tasty rice dish made with vegetables,seasonings,and sometimes liver or chicken.it is simple,and perfect for family meals or special occasions.

Ingredients
  • Rice
  • Carrot
  • Green beans
  • Sweet corn
  • Green pepper
  • Spring onions
  • Liver(optional)
  • Vegetable oil
  • Curry powder
  • Seasonings cubes
  • Salt
  • Water

Steps
  1. Wash the rice.
  2. Cook the rice and set aside.
  3. Stir-fry the vegetables with oil and seasonings.
  4. Add the rice and mix well.
  5. Cook for a few minutes and serve hot.
